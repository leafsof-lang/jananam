Data Policy & Deletion

Jananam Pregnancy Tracker

Last Updated & Effective Date: November 24, 2026

1. Introduction & Principles

This Data Policy explains how Jananam Pregnancy Tracker (developed by Leafsof Technologies) handles data storage, data minimization, and user data deletion requests. We are committed to transparency and empowering users with direct, absolute control over their sensitive maternal and family health records.

2. Data We Process

We process only data entered voluntarily by you to facilitate pregnancy tracking:

  • Pregnancy Setup: Estimated due date, last menstrual period, mom's nickname, baby's nickname.
  • Health Logs: Daily symptoms, hydration glasses, mood, maternal weight, blood pressure readings.
  • Activity Records: Contraction durations and intervals, kick counter timestamps.
  • Doctor Appointments: Scheduled visit dates, clinic notes, and doctor discussion points.

3. How We Store Your Data

Jananam employs an offline-first storage model. Your tracking data is saved locally on your device in secure app-sandboxed storage. We do not transmit or store your personal health entries on our remote servers.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data.
  • We do not share your health data with advertising networks, third-party analytics trackers, or data brokers.
  • Third-party processors (such as Razorpay for voluntary donations/upgrades) process transactions via their own PCI-DSS compliant secure infrastructure. Jananam never stores credit card or bank credentials.

5. Security Standards

All data stored within the app is safeguarded by Android's application sandbox security, preventing unauthorized access from other applications on your device. Any necessary external network requests (such as fetching app updates) use secure HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because your data is stored on your device, you have complete authority over its lifecycle:

How to Delete Your Data
  1. Instant In-App Clearing: You can clear your profile, reset dates, or delete individual symptoms and appointment notes within the app settings or dashboard at any time.
  2. Device Uninstallation: Uninstalling the Jananam Pregnancy Tracker application immediately and permanently wipes all local logs, databases, and stored settings from your device.
  3. Email Deletion Request: If you have communicated with customer support or wish to ensure any email inquiries or correspondence are removed, send an email to leafsof@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my Jananam data".

All written deletion requests are confirmed and permanently fulfilled within 30 days of receipt.

7. Medical Disclaimer

Jananam is an educational and lifestyle tracking tool. It is not a diagnostic device or a substitute for medical supervision. Always seek the advice of your physician or qualified obstetric healthcare provider with any medical questions.
