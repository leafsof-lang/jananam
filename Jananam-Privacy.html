Privacy Policy

Jananam Pregnancy Tracker

Last Updated & Effective Date: November 24, 2026

🔒 100% Local-First & Private by Design Jananam is built with a local-first architecture. Your pregnancy profile, symptoms, kick records, vitals, and doctor appointment notes are saved directly on your local device. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your maternal health data.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Jananam Pregnancy Tracker ("Jananam", "we", "us", or "our"), an educational wellness companion and personal habit tracker developed by Leafsof Technologies. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal and health tracking information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

To help you track your pregnancy milestones and well-being, Jananam allows you to record:

  • Pregnancy Profile Information: Mom's chosen name/nickname, baby's nickname, Last Menstrual Period (LMP) date, conception date, or doctor-assigned estimated due date.
  • Daily Logs & Symptoms: Daily mood selections, logged symptoms (e.g., morning sickness, fatigue, backache), daily water intake, and prenatal vitamin reminders.
  • Vitals & Physical Metrics: Maternal weight measurements and blood pressure readings entered by you.
  • Fetal Activity Logs: Kick counter timestamp sessions and labor contraction timer duration and intervals.
  • Healthcare Visit Records: Scheduled doctor appointment dates, provider names, clinic locations, and personalized questions prepared for your OB-GYN or midwife.
  • Device & Performance Telemetry: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic data such as app crash logs, device model, operating system version, and general performance indicators to ensure app stability.

3. Strict Maternal & Health Data Protection

In accordance with applicable privacy laws and Google Play's Health Apps Policies:

  • No Sale of Data: We never sell, lease, commercialize, or share your reproductive, gestational, or medical tracking data with third-party data brokers, insurers, or marketing networks.
  • No Mandatory Account Creation: You do not need to register an account, link a social media identity, or provide a phone number to track your pregnancy in Jananam.
  • Local Data Storage: All profile settings and daily entries reside directly inside your device's sandboxed local storage (Capacitor/localStorage).

4. How We Use Your Information

We process information strictly to provide and improve the functionality of the Jananam application:

  • Calculating your current gestational week, days elapsed, and estimated delivery countdown.
  • Displaying tailored week-by-week fetal growth milestones and transparent fruit size comparisons.
  • Presenting weekly wellness insights, maternal body changes, and trimester guidance.
  • Scheduling local on-device reminders for scheduled doctor visits and hydration goals.
  • Generating personal symptom and health summary views for your own personal reference.

5. Third-Party Services & Permissions

Jananam may interface with the following trusted third-party service providers:

  • Google Play Services: Used for distributing app updates and validating app integrity.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) & Local Notifications: Used solely to trigger timely local reminders (such as upcoming doctor visits and hydration prompts) on your device.
  • Razorpay: If you choose to unlock optional premium features or support development, payment transactions are processed directly by Razorpay in compliance with PCI-DSS standards. Jananam does not collect, access, or store your credit/debit card numbers or banking passwords.

7. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You maintain complete ownership and control over your data:

  • Instant In-App Reset: You can clear all logs, reset your pregnancy profile, or erase appointment history directly in the app at any time.
  • Device Uninstall: Uninstalling the Jananam app permanently erases all locally stored tracking logs from your device storage.
  • Written Deletion Request: If you have contacted developer support or wish to verify complete data erasure, you can email us at leafsof@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Jananam data". Any support tickets or associated correspondence will be permanently purged within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Jananam is intended for expecting parents and adult caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable local age threshold). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately for deletion.

9. Security Practices

We implement industry-standard security safeguards to protect your information. Local application data is isolated within the Android sandboxed storage container, preventing unauthorized access by other installed applications. All external network communications (such as app update checks) are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
